04 / Bingo! Mad Angles

Making a snack behave like fan culture.

A FIFA content strategy built around rivalry, reactive moments, fan-page behaviour and participation, designed to make the brand participate in culture instead of simply borrowing football aesthetics.

The problem

Football culture is already crowded with memes, creators, fan pages, group chats, rivalries and second-screen behaviour. A brand can look topical and still feel completely outside the culture.

The brand did not need more football posts. It needed a recognisable way to behave during football.

Strategic principle

The opportunity was to use Mad Angles as the chaotic-neutral friend inside fandom, fuelling rivalry rather than choosing a side.

→

Reflection

Keep: build brands around repeatable behaviours, not isolated topical posts.

Change: with live performance access, define a response matrix by match type, rivalry intensity and format speed.

Test next: creator-led rivalry content versus brand-handle reactive formats for comment depth and share rate.
