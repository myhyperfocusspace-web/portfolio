01 / Sunfeast Dark Fantasy

Making taste visible before trial.

An amplification strategy built around a specific consumer tension: dark cues could imply bitterness before trial, but directly saying "not bitter" would drag the brand into the wrong conversation.

What this case proves

Consumer insight, strategy, amplification planning, creator thinking and the ability to carry an idea from deck logic into execution.

The real problem

Dark Fantasy already had a premium, indulgent brand world. The challenge was not to rebuild the brand. It was to recruit aware non-trialists where the name, dark pack history and dark crème could create a bitterness perception.

PerceptionDark → bitter?
vs
ExperienceBite → pleasure.
People did not need a defence of the cookie. They needed visible evidence of pleasure.

The strategic move

A direct rebuttal would repeat the negative frame. The stronger route was to make human behaviour persuasive before the product explanation arrived.

BarrierDark cues can imply bitterness
→
PrincipleDo not argue with the perception
→
Creative opportunityShow involuntary pleasure as social proof

Expression Contagion

The strategic territory became what the work framed as Expression Contagion: visible reactions make taste socially legible.

Reaction-led campaign frame
01

Hook with behaviour

Open on a reaction or pattern that viewers want explained.

02

Build curiosity

Let the audience wonder what caused the reaction instead of making the taste claim immediately.

03

Resolve through Choco Fills

The product becomes the payoff, not the lecture.

From strategy to formats

The principle needed several social expressions so the idea could behave like a system rather than one execution.

The credible outcome is approval and execution: the route moved beyond presentation logic into final creative assets. I am intentionally not attaching unverified reach, sales or conversion metrics.

Reflection

Keep: start with the actual consumer tension, then solve it without damaging the brand tone.

Change: if performance data were available, compare reaction-led hooks with product-first appetite hooks.

Test next: creator-led reaction formats across lower-penetration markets and different completion-rate windows.
