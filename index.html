01

SUNFEAST DARK FANTASY / JUICELABS.AI

Making taste visible before trial.

A consumer-perception problem translated into an amplification system built around visible pleasure, social hooks and creator thinking.

THE PROBLEM

Dark cues could imply bitterness before trial. Repeating "not bitter" would keep the brand trapped inside the wrong frame.

THE MOVE

Do not argue with the perception. Make pleasure visible enough that the audience understands the taste before the explanation arrives.

MY ROLE

Strategy, consumer-barrier translation, amplification routes, social hooks, creator thinking and execution development.

How the thinking moved
01Barrier

Dark visual language can read as intense or bitter.

→
02Behaviour

Human reactions communicate taste faster than a rational defence.

→
03System

Reaction-first hooks, curiosity, creator formats and product payoff.

Boundary: I am not claiming authorship of the master TVC or unverified campaign performance numbers. This case focuses on the amplification work around the campaign.
