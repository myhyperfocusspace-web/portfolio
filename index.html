A little about me

Creative direction, with operator instincts.

I started with content and scripts, learned campaign thinking in agency environments, then moved into GenAI production and enterprise brand work. The common thread has always been the same: take something ambiguous, give it shape, and keep it moving until it becomes real.

I care about creative quality, but I also care about the less glamorous part: clear ownership, usable feedback, product accuracy, timelines and making sure the final output still reflects the original idea.
