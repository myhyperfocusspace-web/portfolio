01
SUNFEAST DARK FANTASY / JUICELABS.AI

Making taste visible before trial.

A consumer-perception problem translated into an amplification system built around visible pleasure, short-form hooks and creator thinking.
VIEW CASE STUDY ↗

PERCEPTION

“Dark” could suggest bitterness.

The challenge was not awareness. It was the possibility that dark visual cues created the wrong expectation before someone even tried the product.

INSIGHT

Do not defend the product.

Repeating “not bitter” would keep the brand inside a negative frame. Human behaviour could communicate indulgence faster than a rational rebuttal.

IDEA

Make pleasure visible.

Reaction-first hooks, curiosity and product payoff turned the strategic thought into an amplification system instead of one isolated post.

AASHIRVAAD SOYA CHUNKS × SUNRISE / JUICELABS.AI

Turning one TVC into a reusable promo system.

A 15-second adaptation where motion, pack hierarchy, VO, text and the offer had to keep changing without breaking the underlying film.
VIEW CASE STUDY ↗

01THE MASTER FILM

Already had the appetite.

The product story and food beauty were working. The adaptation needed to preserve that equity rather than restart the film as an offer ad.

02THE CHALLENGE

Introduce an offer without breaking the film.

The gravy drip became a visual bridge, changing into the red-gold ribbon that could carry the promotional world naturally into frame.

03THE SOLUTION

A modular promotional ending system.

Sunrise enters as the offer support, the pack grouping and supers follow the VO, and Aashirvaad closes as the hero. The same structure can support future promotional swaps.
