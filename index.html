About

Creative direction with operator instincts.

I started with content and scripts, learned campaign thinking in agency environments, moved into GenAI production, and then into client-facing creative leadership. The common thread is simple: take something unclear, give it shape, and keep it moving until it becomes real.

I care about the idea, but also the less glamorous layer around it: clear ownership, usable feedback, product accuracy, timelines and whether the final output still contains the original intention.
