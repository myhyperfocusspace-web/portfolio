The brief

Create ecommerce product storytelling across Mapro and Mazzaana, covering both appetite-building communication and the information-heavy layers a PDP needs.

The work had to span different products without looking like a random collection of generated images.

The job was not to make one beautiful visual. It was to make a system that could keep producing useful ones.

What I owned

Creative production

  • Generated product heroes and lifestyle frames
  • Built ingredient, texture and usage stories
  • Created nutrition, regulatory and pack-information frames
  • Produced cross-sell and range visuals

Operational layer

  • Handled repeated client feedback rounds
  • Protected pack and product fidelity
  • Worked across several SKUs in parallel
  • Delivered while managing other JuiceLabs.ai projects

Product accuracy mattered

GenAI made the production faster, but it also introduced a different kind of risk. Packaging, ingredient cues, weights, information and product anatomy could not simply be treated as decorative detail.

Outcome

A repeatable AI commerce-production workflow across multiple product families.

The strongest outcome is the system itself: generated visual production that could move from hero imagery to proof, information and cross-sell without losing the product as the centre of the communication.

Reflection

Keep: the PDP logic. Hero, proof, desire and information creates a reusable production grammar.

Change: formalise a stricter pre-generation pack-reference sheet and final QA checklist for weights, ingredients, legal text and pack geometry.

Test next: build master prompt systems by category so new SKUs can enter the same visual world faster without starting from zero.
