The brief

Repurpose an existing Aashirvaad Soya Chunks TVC into a reusable promotional format for a free Sunrise Garam Masala ₹5 pack.

This was not a blank-page campaign. The existing product story, mnemonic and food footage needed to remain intact while the offer became clear enough to own the last seconds of the film.

The promo needed to feel like the TVC naturally became an offer, not like an offer interrupted the TVC.

Production reality

The strongest part of this project is not that everything went smoothly. It is that the work remained deliverable while the requirements continued to change.

What changed

  • Storyboard alignment after approval
  • Dummy VO introduced mid-process
  • Background style and brand colours
  • On-screen text direction
  • Regular pack vs mini pack requirement
  • 5g product accuracy correction
  • Animation language, roughly six routes explored
  • Bengali VO approval timing
  • Jingle and SFX hierarchy

What I handled

  • Asset regeneration
  • Pack and offer corrections
  • Motion exploration
  • VO and text synchronisation
  • Feedback translation
  • Production blockers and rework
  • Final delivery discipline

Feedback became decisions

The project involved detailed feedback around product hierarchy, drip colour, pack reveal, ribbon smoothness, jingle timing, VO, offer supers and background direction.

Outcome

A changing production process still ended in a usable system.

The final film and reusable promotional template were delivered on schedule. The case proves adaptive project leadership more than a single isolated creative idea.

Reflection

Keep: modular thinking. A fixed transition, colour, motion and end-frame system makes future promo swaps easier.

Change: lock motion reference, pack configuration, brand palette and VO language earlier through one pre-production checklist.

Test next: build a small pre-approved motion library so future offer variants do not reopen style exploration.
